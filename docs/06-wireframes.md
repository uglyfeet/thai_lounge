# Wireframes — The Thai Lounge

## 1. Purpose

The wireframes describe the planned layout of the main website pages before development begins. The design will be simple, consistent and responsive.

## 2. Shared Page Layout

All pages will use a consistent layout containing:

- A header with the business name and navigation.
- A main content area.
- Clear buttons and form labels.
- A footer containing contact information and relevant links.

The navigation will provide access to the homepage, treatments, bookings and account pages. Administrative links will only be available to authorised users.

## 3. Homepage

The homepage will contain:

- The Thai Lounge name and welcome message.
- A suitable image representing the business.
- A short introduction to the service.
- A prominent button to view treatments or book an appointment.
- A section highlighting available treatments.

## 4. Treatments Page

The treatments page will display treatments in individual cards. Each card will contain:

- Treatment name.
- Description.
- Duration.
- Price.
- A button to book the treatment.

## 5. Registration and Login Pages

These pages will contain clearly labelled forms for customer registration and login. Validation errors will be displayed clearly.

## 6. Booking Page

The booking page will allow a logged-in customer to:

- Select a treatment.
- Choose an appointment date.
- Select an available time.
- Review the booking details and price.
- Continue to payment.

Unavailable appointment times will not be offered.

## 7. Customer Bookings Page

This page will display the customer's bookings, including treatment, date, time, booking status and payment status where appropriate. Customers will be able to access the permitted actions for their own bookings.

## 8. Administration Pages

The business owner will use a protected administration area to manage treatments, availability and bookings.

## 9. Responsive Design

The website will adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens. Navigation, treatment cards and forms will remain readable and usable on smaller screens.

## 10. Design Status

These are initial layout plans. The final implementation may be adjusted during development and testing to meet the assessment requirements and improve usability.
