# User Personas — The Thai Lounge

## Persona 1: The Customer

**Name:** Sarah

**Role:** Customer looking for a Thai massage treatment.

**Goals:**
- Find suitable massage treatments and compare prices.
- Check treatment durations and appointment availability.
- Book an appointment online at a convenient time.
- Pay securely and receive booking confirmation.
- View or cancel an existing booking.

**Needs:**
- A clear, easy-to-use website.
- Accurate treatment information and availability.
- A straightforward booking and payment process.
- Secure access to personal booking information.

**Potential difficulties:**
- Unclear prices or treatment descriptions.
- Appointment times that are unavailable.
- Confusing forms or payment errors.

## Persona 2: The Business Owner

**Name:** The Thai Lounge owner

**Role:** Manages the massage business and customer appointments.

**Goals:**
- Maintain treatment descriptions, durations and prices.
- Control appointment availability.
- View, update and manage bookings.
- Check booking and payment statuses.
- Reduce time spent arranging appointments manually.

**Needs:**
- Secure administrative access.
- Reliable booking information.
- Simple tools for managing treatments and appointments.
- Clear information about successful and unsuccessful payments.

**Potential difficulties:**
- Conflicting appointments.
- Incorrect booking information.
- Unclear payment status.
- Difficulty updating treatments or availability.

## Summary

The website must provide a straightforward booking experience for customers and practical management tools for the business owner. Security, accurate availability and clear payment feedback are important to both users.
